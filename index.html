How to play

Simple to learn, satisfying to master — true to that Block Blast feel.

01

Place pieces

Drag colorful blocks onto the 8×8 grid. Rotate is automatic in your head — fit them where they belong.

02

Fill rows & columns

Complete a full horizontal row or vertical column and it clears instantly for points.

03

Chase combos

Clear multiple lines in one move for combo bonuses and a rising score.

04

Daily challenge

A fresh set of goals every day — play offline, anytime.

Daily challenge

Two fresh goals every day, always of different kinds. Progress is tracked locally, offline.

Clear Blocks

Clear X blocks (120, 150, 200, 250, 300). Counts every cell removed by line clears that day.

Earn Points

Earn X points (3000, 4000, 5000, 6000, 8000). Accumulates points earned across play that day.

Place a Named Piece

Place 8, 10, 12 or 15 of a named family - L, squares, T, bars, S or Z. Rotations count.

Combo

Get a 3, 3 or 4 combo. Feat goal (target 1): completes if any run reaches the required streak.

Multi-Line Clear

Clear 2, 2 or 3 lines at once. Feat goal (target 1): one placement clears at least that many lines.

Score in Multiple Runs

Score 500 / 600 / 600 / 800 in 3, 3 or 4 runs. Progress +1 each run that hits the target, credited immediately.
